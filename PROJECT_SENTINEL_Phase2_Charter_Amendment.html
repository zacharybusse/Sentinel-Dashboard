3.5 Contingency Activation Process

  1. Risk Threshold Exceeded: Risk owner or Project Manager identifies threshold exceedance
  2. PM Escalation: Project Manager escalates to Executive Sponsor within 24 hours
  3. Sponsor Notification: Sponsor notifies Finance Director and PMO
  4. Financial Assessment: Finance analyzes impact
    • <$100K: PM + Finance approval (1-2 days)
    • >$100K: Board vote required (emergency meeting if needed)
  5. Contingency Decision: Board approves or defers contingency activation
  6. Authorization: If approved, authorized work begins immediately
  7. Tracking: Contingency spend tracked monthly against board oversight
